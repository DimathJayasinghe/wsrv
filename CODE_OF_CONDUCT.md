# Code of Conduct

This project follows the standards of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
which is incorporated here by reference. The short version:

## Our standard
Be respectful and constructive. Welcome people of every background and level of experience.
Give and accept technical criticism of the work, never of the person. Assume good faith, and
say plainly when you disagree.

Not acceptable: harassment or discrimination of any kind, sexualised language or attention,
personal or political attacks, publishing other people's private information, trolling, and
anything else that would be out of place in a professional setting.

## Scope
This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, commit
messages) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and contributions that do not
follow this code, and may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently ban a person for behaviour
they judge inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

**To report a problem**, use the repository's Security tab, "Report a vulnerability" (a private
report only the maintainers can read), and say at the top that it is a conduct report. Reports are
handled privately, the reporter's identity is kept confidential, and a maintainer who is involved
in the matter does not take part in deciding it.

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant's guidelines: a correction, a warning, a temporary
ban, a permanent ban, in that order of seriousness, chosen by the impact of the behaviour.
